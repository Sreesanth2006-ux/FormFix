About this prototype

This is a working click-through of the FormFix AI experience: exercise selection, a live camera view with a simulated skeleton overlay, real-time coaching feedback, and a post-session recap.

The camera feed is real (your browser's webcam). The pose analysis and coaching text are scripted for this demo — the production build replaces this with on-device pose estimation (e.g. MoveNet) feeding an LLM coaching layer.
